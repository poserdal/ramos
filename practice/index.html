Practice.

Langford | Ramos represents people facing criminal investigations and charges in state and federal court.

The firm is deliberately focused. It handles serious criminal matters from investigation through trial.

Federal Criminal Defense.

Langford | Ramos represents people facing federal investigation and prosecution. The firm handles federal matters from pre-charge investigation through indictment, motions, trial, and sentencing.

State Criminal Defense.

The firm represents people charged in Utah state courts in serious felony and misdemeanor matters. Representation may begin before charges are filed and continue through trial.

Investigations

Langford | Ramos represents people facing criminal investigation before formal charges are filed. That work may include contact with law enforcement, subpoenas, search warrants, and other pre-charge matters.
